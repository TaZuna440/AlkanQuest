# AlkanQuest — User Manual

**Level up your savings.** AlkanQuest turns saving money into Solo Leveling-style quests. You create savings quests, log check-ins when you save, earn XP, climb ranks from E to S, and unlock achievements.

> AlkanQuest is a habit tracker, **not** a bank, wallet, or investment app. Nobody verifies your savings — you report them honestly to yourself.

---

## 1. First launch — Awakening (Onboarding)

1. Open the app. The **System Window** boots with a short intro.
2. **Registration** — pick your hunter display name (up to 24 characters).
3. **First Quest** — the System offers a starter quest template (e.g. "₱1,000 Emergency Fund"). Accept it or skip; you can create your own anytime.
4. **Briefing** — a quick rundown of the rules. Then the dashboard opens.

## 2. Create a quest

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap **+ NEW QUEST**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Quest name** (e.g. "New Phone", "Emergency Fund")
   - **Target amount** in pesos (₱)
   - **Target date** — when you want to finish
   - **Frequency** — daily, weekly, monthly, or flexible. The app greys out frequencies that don't fit your date range.
   - Optional: starting amount (already saved), notes, icon color.
3. The **plan card** tells you exactly what to save per day/week/month (rounded up: ₱10 daily, ₱50 weekly, ₱100 monthly).
4. Tap **CREATE QUEST**. It appears on your dashboard with a progress bar.

## 3. Turn on reminders (recommended)

1. Go to the **Profile** tab → **DAILY REMINDERS** → toggle on.
2. The app explains why, then asks the system's permission. Allow it.
3. Pick your **REMINDER TIME** (default 8:00 PM). The System nudges you once a day.

## 4. Log a check-in ("I saved it!")

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap a quest card (or its **+ I SAVED IT** button).
2. Enter **how much you saved** in pesos. Quick-pick chips suggest amounts based on your plan.
3. Optionally add a **note** ("skipped milk tea") and a **proof photo**.
4. Tap **CONFIRM SAVINGS**.
5. The System reports back:
   - **+50 XP** — only for your *first* check-in of the day on that quest (extra check-ins still count toward your total, just without XP).
   - **LEVEL UP / RANK UP** when you cross thresholds.
   - **[ QUEST COMPLETE ] +500 XP** when you hit the target.
   - **[ ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED ]** celebrations (FIRST IPON, FIRST 1,000, etc.).

> If you close the check-in screen halfway, your half-filled form is kept as a **draft** and restored next time.

## 5. Read your dashboard

- **Rank badge + level** — ranks go E → D → C → B → A → S (S starts at 25,000 XP).
- **XP bar** — shows progress to the next rank.
- **Total saved, daily streak, quests cleared in a row.**
- Each quest card shows: progress bar, saved/target, **remaining amount**, **suggested amount per period**, and its **streak** (🔥 consecutive days/weeks/months with a check-in).

## 6. Quest detail

Tap any quest card to open its detail window:

- Progress, stats, and the full **check-in history** (tap a proof thumbnail to view it).
- **EDIT QUEST** — change name, notes, target, date, or frequency. The target can't go below what's already saved; the plan recalculates automatically. A past-due quest is flagged **OVERDUE**.
- **CANCEL QUEST** — abandons the quest. Its records stay in history, but your quests-cleared streak resets to 0. (Asks for confirmation.)
- **DELETE QUEST** — permanently removes the quest *and* its records. (Asks for confirmation.)

## 7. Status, Achievements, Profile tabs

- **Status** — big rank card, lifetime stats (totals, quest counts, streaks), and your **XP timeline** (every +XP event, newest first).
- **Quests** (achievements) — all 6 achievements, gold when unlocked, with progress hints while locked ("₱350 / ₱1,000 saved").
- **Profile** — edit your name, switch **language** (English/Filipino), toggle **System voice** (dramatic Solo Leveling flavor vs plain wording), manage reminders, and **export your data** as CSV.

## 8. XP, ranks & streaks cheat sheet

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| First check-in of the day (per quest) | +50 |
| Quest completed | +500 |
| 7 quests cleared in a row | +200 |
| 30 quests cleared in a row | +1,000 |
| Achievements (FIRST IPON, FIRST 1,000, …) | +100 – +1,500 |

| Rank | XP | Title |
|---|---|---|
| E | 0 – 499 | Ipon Beginner |
| D | 500 – 1,499 | Consistent Saver |
| C | 1,500 – 3,999 | Challenge Hunter |
| B | 4,000 – 9,999 | Savings Warrior |
| A | 10,000 – 24,999 | Ipon Master |
| S | 25,000+ | Legendary Saver |

- **Quest streak** — consecutive days/weeks/months (by quest frequency) with at least one check-in.
- **Daily streak** — consecutive days you checked in on *any* quest.
- **Cleared streak** — quests completed in a row. Cancelling a quest resets it to 0.

## 9. FAQ / Troubleshooting

**I didn't get my daily reminder.**
→ Profile tab → make sure DAILY REMINDERS is on. If you declined the permission twice, Android won't ask again — enable notifications for AlkanQuest in your phone's Settings.

**My quest says OVERDUE.**
→ The target date passed before you hit the target. Edit the quest to set a new date, or keep checking in — progress still counts.

**I logged the wrong amount.**
→ Open the quest detail → check-in history. (Deleting individual records is not in this version — delete and re-log, or delete the quest and start over. The team notes this as a possible improvement.)

**Will I lose data if I uninstall?**
→ Yes. Everything lives on your device. Use **EXPORT DATA** in the Profile tab to keep CSV backups of your quests and check-ins.

**The app is in English — can I switch?**
→ Profile tab → LANGUAGE → FIL. Key screens, buttons, and System messages switch to Filipino.
